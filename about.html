A practical path
into applied AI.

I’m Mehul Pandey, an AI engineer working across Generative AI, retrieval, machine learning and backend systems. I’m interested in the engineering that carries a model from a promising idea into a dependable product workflow.

How I got here

My foundation is a B.Tech (Honours) in Computer Science Engineering — Data Science from CSVTU. During research at the Indian Institute of Space Science and Technology, I worked with ECG signals, HRV features and classical machine-learning models to study stress detection.

From research I moved into product-oriented engineering: first backend modules and AI integrations at 4th Orbit, then broader AI and backend responsibilities at Toss Solutions. Across that work I’ve built APIs, asynchronous processing flows, retrieval systems, document workflows and computer-vision quality checks.

I like to understand the whole path: how data enters, how a model or retrieval step changes it, how a service handles the work, and where a person should verify the result.

How I work

  • 01Frame the problem and define a useful output.
  • 02Design the data, model and service boundaries.
  • 03Build the backend workflow and integrate the AI component.
  • 04Evaluate with the available data and make limitations visible.
  • 05Keep a human review path where the task needs one.

Current focus

  • LLM applications, private model adaptation and RAG
  • Semantic search, ranking and relevance evaluation
  • OCR, VLM and reviewable document workflows
  • Distributed backend systems for data-heavy AI tasks
  • Applied machine learning for signals and computer vision
